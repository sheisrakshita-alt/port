About Me

I’m Nishant Suhas Bhoir, a video editor who loves turning raw footage into meaningful, memorable stories. I work across the full editing process—from organizing footage and shaping a clear narrative to refining pacing, adding thoughtful transitions, and finishing with considered color grading and motion design.

My approach is equal parts creative and detail-focused: every cut should serve the story, every visual choice should feel intentional, and the final video should connect with its audience. Whether I’m working on a brand promo, a documentary, a music video, or short-form social content, I aim to deliver polished work that feels engaging, cohesive, and true to the project’s vision.
